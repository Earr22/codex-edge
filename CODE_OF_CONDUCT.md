# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's credentials or private browser data are not accepted.

When reporting failures, remove usernames, local paths, account pages, tab titles, screenshots, tokens, cookies, and private URLs. Maintainers may remove content that creates a privacy or security risk.

Project maintainers may moderate contributions and discussions to protect participants and keep the project focused.
